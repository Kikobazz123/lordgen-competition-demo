## Problem

Industry-wide, dental practices lose potential patients when the phone line is busy (often tied up with insurance checks), and inefficient scheduling systems cause double-booking and underutilized appointment slots. These are verified, sourced industry statistics — not findings about any specific clinic.

## Proposed AI Solution

An AI-assisted scheduling/call-overflow step: when the line is busy or it's after hours, an AI step captures the patient's request and offers available slots or a callback — instead of the call simply going unanswered. Structurally identical to the Nick's Plumbing build — AI drafts/handles the routine case, a human stays in the loop for anything that needs it.

## Why This Matters

93% of the highest-satisfaction patients cite easy communication as the reason — this directly targets the mechanism (a busy or unanswered line) that breaks that experience, without proposing a new booking system or workflow overhaul.
