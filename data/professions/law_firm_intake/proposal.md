# LordGen Solution Profile — Law Firm Intake (Seeded Profession)

*This is a seeded, generic profession profile — not a proposal for a specific named firm. Generated to demonstrate that LordGen's scoring/proposal skills generalize across professions, using the same rubric applied to Nick's Plumbing & Air Conditioning (`proposal.md`, kept local/unpublished — see `docs/professions.md`).*

## Problem

Industry-wide, solo and small law firms lose a significant share of prospective clients to slow or missed responses: one in three calls goes unanswered, 70% of firms lack a structured follow-up process, and the firm that responds first wins the client roughly 68% of the time. These are verified, sourced industry statistics — not findings about any specific firm.

## Proposed AI Solution

An AI-assisted intake triage step: every inbound lead (phone or web form) is answered/logged immediately, a same-day follow-up is drafted, and anything needing an attorney's direct judgment is flagged rather than auto-handled. Structurally identical to the Nick's Plumbing build — AI drafts, a human approves before anything client-facing goes out.

## Why This Matters

The evidence here is unusually strong and quantified for the *industry as a whole* — but this profile stops at the proposal stage deliberately. Turning this into an execution plan and automation would require researching an actual firm, the same way Nick's Plumbing was researched, rather than reusing generic statistics.
