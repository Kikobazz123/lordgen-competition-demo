                  Frequently Asked Questions

                  Is this a real AI system, or a mockup?

                  Both live on this page. The "Live Systems" demo above is a client-side simulation of the same workflow shape LordGen built and tested as a real n8n automation. The Business Diagnostic's plumbing preset is wired to that real, running workflow.

                  What does "Prototype" mean on this page?

                  It means the business shown is illustrative and the workflow is demonstrated, not yet running for a real paying client. We label every prototype honestly rather than presenting it as a finished case study.

                  Does the Business Diagnostic use my real data?

                  No. The diagnostic runs against the pre-set demo businesses only. No visitor-submitted data is sent anywhere in this competition build.

                  What tools does LordGen connect to?

                  Depends on the business, but commonly a CRM, email/SMS, calendars, and forms, orchestrated through n8n with an AI layer for analysis and drafting.

                  How does human approval work?

                  Every AI-drafted action that goes external — a reply, a booking confirmation — is held for human approval before it sends. That gate is not optional and is visible throughout this demo.

                  What happens after I run the diagnostic?

                  You see the detected opportunity, choose which parts to build, and get a concrete automation specification — trigger, inputs, steps, and integrations — that a developer can inspect and implement.
